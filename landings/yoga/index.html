Find your silence above

the clouds

We believe that true healing happens when you step away from the noise and return to something ancient — the mountains, the breath, the stillness within.

Our studio was born from a simple desire: to create a space where yoga is not a workout, but a homecoming. Here, surrounded by peaks and pines, you won't find crowded classes or bright mirrors. You'll find silence, support, and the space to simply be.

Whether you're new to the mat or have been practicing for years, you're welcome to move at your own pace — guided by the mountains, held by the practice.
